# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and kind, assume good intent, disagree about ideas and never about people, and leave
harassment, insults and personal attacks out entirely.

To report behaviour that breaks it, contact the maintainers through
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/escalay/yardhand/security/advisories/new) marked "conduct", or
GitHub's own reporting on the comment or issue. Reports are kept confidential, and maintainers may remove content
and block participants who don't follow it.
