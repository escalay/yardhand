## An app asked to sign in at a pack's address is pointed at that address

- **The 401 at `/p/<pack>/mcp` now points at `/.well-known/oauth-protected-resource/p/<pack>/mcp`**, whose `resource` is the address the app asked for. Before, it pointed at the gateway's own `/mcp` metadata, and apps that check the two match (RFC 9728), such as Claude Code, stopped there: "Protected resource …/mcp does not match expected …".
- **Signing in to a pack from an app is not finished yet.** An app the owner approves at a pack's address is still answered "This key isn't locked to packs: connect to /mcp" there; locking an app's sign-in to one pack comes with the kit update in a later release. Pack keys from Share work as before.

Nothing to do by hand.
